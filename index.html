I'm a Computer Engineering student with a passion for building things that bridge the physical and digital world. From designing PCB layouts for solar-powered greenhouses to developing Android apps with clean MVVM architecture — I like the full stack, hardware to software.

My undergraduate thesis, the S3-RAD, is a solar-powered, IoT-enabled laundry drying system with sun-tracking and rain detection — rated 4.72/5.0 under ISO 25010 evaluation. I also love security research: my group built an IoT Vulnerability Scanner with automated hardening recommendations.

Outside of tech, I've worked as an Account Manager in a BPO setting, which gave me solid communication and client management chops that complement my engineering side.
